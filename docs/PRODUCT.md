# Product

ANGON is a tourism- and culture-focused social platform: *a social network for places, people & stories.* It is not a booking marketplace, a Facebook/Instagram clone or a generic blog CMS.

## Content concepts
- **Post** — short social content: text, photos/video, location, hashtags; like, comment, save.
- **Story** — long-form editorial writing with cover, rich content, location, tags.
- **Place** — a destination page aggregating posts, stories, photos and creators.
- **Profile** — a creator/traveller identity: followers, posts, stories, saved items, places documented.

## Themes
Travel · Culture · Heritage · People · Places · Nature · Food · Photography · Stories · Community.

## Design principles
Editorial, warm, visual, calm. Strong typography, photography and whitespace; subtle motion; no heavy gradients or glassmorphism. Bengali and English are first-class (Newsreader/Inter with Noto Bengali fallbacks).

## Long-term vision
People → Places → Stories → Culture → Communities → Experiences → Tourism. Bookings, payments, hotels and AI are explicitly out of scope until later phases.
